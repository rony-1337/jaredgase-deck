## Why this one

I am a dad of three, and I build and tinker with connected devices at home, so a
family-safety wearable is a problem I actually care about. This was a
self-directed exercise to stretch into hardware plus software and the full
lifecycle of a physical device.

## What it covers

The design walks the whole arc: pairing and setup, onboarding a nervous parent,
the everyday locate flow, and the panic moment of reporting a child lost. The
interesting technical problem is range. When a child wanders out of Bluetooth
range, the band falls back to ultra-wideband and a crowdsourced beacon network,
the same idea behind how Tile, AirTags, and Life360 keep working when the device
is far away.

This is the piece I reach for when a role is about consumer products, connected
devices, family safety, or zero-to-one work where the physical and digital have
to feel like one product.
