## The situation

Concierge began life as a simple scheduling tool. Useful, but narrow. The bigger
opportunity was sitting right there: the moments when a customer first connects
with an expert are exactly the moments that decide whether they activate and
convert. That was a growth system waiting to be designed, not just a utility to
maintain.

## What I did

I helped grow Concierge from that scheduling tool into a connected, end to end
experience that supports both inbound and outbound engagement at scale. Across
versions 2, 2.5, 3, and 3.5, I shaped a coherent system rather than a pile of
features, and tied each design decision back to a measurable business outcome.

This is a genuine zero-to-one story, and I want to be precise about the credit.
I lead design on the Virtual Expert Platform, the larger platform Concierge
lives inside. I did not build that platform. Concierge is the thing I built up
from the ground.

## Grounded in customer and business reality

Throughout, I anchored decisions in both customer insight and business
constraints. Roundtable sessions and ongoing time with the Concierge teams
surfaced patterns in behavior, friction, and opportunity. I balanced those
against scalability and operational limits, so the solutions were both
customer-centered and actually viable at the program's scale.

## Defining where it goes next

Beyond shipping, I looked ahead. I identified Concierge as a strong entry point
for establishing seller workflows inside IEP, Intuit's broader expert platform,
because it pairs relatively low complexity with high business impact. I drove
early alignment across design, product, and leadership, contributed to the
emerging strategy, and partnered on prototypes that made the future state
tangible enough to rally around.

## The results

Concierge now influences 53 percent of conversions and supports roughly 5,500
expert calls a day, around 3,000 inbound and 2,500 outbound. It is part of a
$250M revenue program, which means the design work connects directly to the
top line, not just to satisfaction scores. The version I helped shape moved it
from a scheduling tool to one of the clearest examples of design driving growth
in the org.

## What I took from it

Concierge is how I show that I can take something small and ambiguous and grow
it into a system that matters to the business. It is the proof point I lead with
for zero-to-one and growth work, and it set up the platform direction the team
is still building toward.
