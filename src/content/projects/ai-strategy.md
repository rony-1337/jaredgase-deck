## The ask

I was asked to serve as the strategic design advisor for AI initiatives across
Sales and Customer Success. That is a deliberate scope expansion beyond my scrum
team, and it reflects a level of trust to shape direction broadly, not just to
execute inside one product.

## What the work is

The core problem is fragmentation. Several teams were building AI into the seller
experience independently, which is how you end up with a dozen disconnected
features instead of one coherent system. My focus is keeping that effort aligned
with a single platform direction and connecting the dots across teams.

As the design lead for this, I presented and helped strategize an AI-First Seller
workshop, bringing a design lens to how AI actually shows up across the seller
journey. I also authored a whitepaper that reframes SalesTech as a service
problem, not a collection of tools, which gave leadership a shared way to think
about the whole system.

## How I operate in it

I identify the dependencies between teams, guide people toward scalable
solutions instead of one-off fixes, and hold a clear, consistent point of view
on how AI should influence product strategy across the ecosystem.

## Why it matters

This is my clearest signal of working at the system level. It is influence
without authority, across two orgs, on the most important technology shift
happening in the product right now.
