## The angle

My career started in brand and marketing design, and that is where my eye for
craft comes from. I moved into product and UX over time, but I still enjoy the
visual and interactive side, and I reach for this work when a role leans brand,
web, design systems, or motion.

## What it includes

At the agency, GDUSA-recognized work and client campaigns with real lift,
including a strong jump in leads and sessions and a big improvement in load time.
In-house at a university system, I rebuilt the application experience, cutting a
14-step flow down to 3, and hand-coded mobile-optimized email campaigns that
moved enrollment numbers. A program-page and tuition-calculator redesign drove a
sharp spike in inquiries in its first week.

It is the proof that the systems and strategy work sits on top of real craft,
not the other way around.
