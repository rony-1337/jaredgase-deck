# Where to drop assets

Drop files into the folders below. If you are not sure where something goes,
put it in `_inbox/` and I will sort and rename it. You do not need to optimize
or resize anything. I will convert to webp and generate the right sizes during
the build. Originals at full resolution are best.

## Naming rules (simple)

1. Lowercase, words joined by hyphens, no spaces. `creative-studio.jpg`, not
   `Creative Studio.JPG`.
2. The folder says the category, the filename says what it is.
3. For anything that appears inside a project scroll, put a two digit number in
   front to tell me the reveal order: `01-...`, `02-...`. First number is the
   first thing revealed as you scroll that section.
4. Any format is fine (jpg, png, webp, svg, mp4). Logos and simple diagrams as
   svg if you have them.
5. If a name is obvious to you it is probably obvious to me. Do not overthink
   it. `_inbox/` is always a safe fallback.
